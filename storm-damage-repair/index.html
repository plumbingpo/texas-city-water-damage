Heavy rainstorms, hurricanes, tropical storms and flash floods can do serious water damage to homes and buildings in Texas City. Wind-driven rain pushes water in through damaged roofing, broken windows and loose siding. Once the roof leaks, water saturates wall cavities, ceilings and insulation. Flash flooding overwhelms drainage systems and pushes water into lower levels. When a storm damages your property, Texas City Restoration responds on an emergency basis to limit water intrusion, extract standing water and start restoration right away.

Our storm damage repair covers the whole job, from emergency board-up and tarping that keep more water out, through water extraction, structural drying and rebuilding what was damaged. One problem tends to trigger the next after a storm, as a roof opening admits water, wet ceilings and walls break down, and poor drying leads to mold growth. We address all of these connected issues under one coordinated restoration plan.

We document everything a claim needs after a storm, including a full scope-of-work report, damage assessments, moisture data and photographs of every affected area. Throughout the claim, our team deals with your insurer directly and can speak up on your behalf so storm-related damage is assessed fairly.

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
