A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Texas City Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Texas City, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Know the location and condition of your sewer cleanout for emergency access
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Texas City

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
