A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Texas City Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Texas City to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Texas City

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
